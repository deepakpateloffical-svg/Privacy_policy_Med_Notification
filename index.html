Privacy Policy for Med Notification

Effective Date: November 6, 2026 | Last Updated: November 6, 2026

Welcome to Med Notification ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and treating your sensitive health-related information with the utmost confidentiality, dignity, and care.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, stores, synchronizes, and discloses your information in compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, GDPR, and relevant data protection regulations.

Medical Disclaimer

Med Notification is an organizational tool designed to assist users in tracking medication schedules and adherence history. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, prescription validation, or clinical recommendations. Never alter, discontinue, or initiate any medication regimen without direct consultation with your qualified physician, healthcare provider, or pharmacist.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on your usage mode (Offline Guest Mode or Cloud-Synchronized Google Account), we collect the following categories of data:

A. Account and Authentication Information

If you choose to enable cloud backup and multi-device synchronization, you sign in securely via Google Sign-In using Android's Jetpack Credential Manager. We receive:

  • Account Identifiers: Your Google user ID, display name, and email address.
  • Profile Picture: Optional avatar URL associated with your Google account.

B. Medication & Prescription Information

Information you explicitly input to manage your regimen:

  • Prescription Details: Medication name, dosage (e.g., 500 mg, 1 tablet), form (pill, capsule, liquid, injection, inhaler, drops), color, shape, and instructions (e.g., with meals, before bed).
  • Schedules & Exact Times: Daily reminder times (e.g., 08:00, 20:00) used by the on-device alarm manager to deliver notification alerts.
  • Supply & Inventory: Remaining pill count, automatic refill threshold, and prescriber/physician name.

C. Adherence Logs & Clinical History

Records generated as you take or log your doses:

  • Dose Status: Status indicator (Taken, Skipped, Snoozed, or Missed).
  • Timestamps: Scheduled dose time and actual recorded intake timestamp.
  • Personal Notes: Optional comments or side-effect observations logged by you or your caregiver.

D. Family Profiles & Caregiver Linkage

  • Family Profiles: Profile names, relationships (Self, Mom, Dad, Child, Spouse, Other), avatar color preferences, and health allergy notes.
  • Caregiver Monitoring: Authorized caregiver names, contact emails, and secure 6-digit access pairing codes (`CARE-XXXX`) generated to grant remote adherence view permissions.

E. Health & Activity Telemetry (Optional)

  • Activity Data: Step counts, hydration intake tracking, active movement minutes, and resting heart rate metrics tracked via the Health Hub when connected by the user.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process the collected data strictly to deliver and enhance the core functionality of Med Notification:

  • Medication Reminders: To schedule on-time push notifications and exact alarms on your device with actionable "Take" and "Snooze" options.
  • Adherence Analytics: To compute your compliance percentage, track adherence streaks, and visualize intake history.
  • Automated Refill Reminders: To alert you when your medication inventory dips below your specified threshold.
  • Doctor Consultation Reports: To generate exportable clinical summaries that you can share with your physician or healthcare provider.
  • Remote Caregiver Dashboard: To allow paired family caregivers to review compliance and receive urgent missed-dose or refill warnings.
  • Multi-Device Cloud Sync: To synchronize your family profiles, medications, and logs securely across your Android devices.

3. Offline Mode & Local Storage

Offline-First Reliability: Med Notification is architected to be 100% functional without an active internet connection. You may use the App in Offline Guest Mode, in which case all medication data, schedules, and logs remain strictly on your local device cache.

4. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

When cloud synchronization is active, we utilize trusted Google Cloud and Firebase services that adhere to stringent international security standards (ISO 27001, SOC 1/2/3, HIPAA-compliant infrastructure):

  • Google Play Services: Core Android runtime libraries, Credential Manager, and notifications. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Authentication: Secure authentication handling via Google Sign-In identity tokens.
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore: Encrypted cloud database utilized for real-time data persistence and caregiver synchronization.
No Advertising or Data Selling: We do NOT sell, rent, license, or monetize your health data, personal logs, or contact details to third-party data brokers, advertisers, or pharmaceutical marketing networks.

5. Data Security Safeguards

We implement defense-in-depth security measures to protect your medical privacy:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your device and Firebase servers is encrypted using modern TLS (Transport Layer Security) cryptographic protocols.
  • Encryption at Rest: Cloud databases and on-device caches are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
  • Zero-Trust Database Security Rules: Rigorous server-side Firestore security rules verify user authentication and guarantee that no user can access, query, or modify another family's data.
  • Biometric & PIN Passcode Lock: You can enable an optional in-app biometric/passcode lock to protect your confidential medication records from unauthorized physical access on your device.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Your Right to Control Your Data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can edit or permanently delete individual medications, logs, caregiver links, and family profiles at any time directly within the application.
  • Complete Account Deletion: You may request complete deletion of your account and all associated cloud records by emailing deepakpateloffical@gmail.com. Upon receipt, your cloud data will be permanently wiped within 30 days in compliance with Google Play Data Deletion requirements.
  • Local Reset: Clearing the App data or uninstalling the application removes all local device caches and stored preferences immediately.

7. Device Permissions Declared

Med Notification requests only the minimum Android system permissions required for its documented features:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to display medication alerts, snooze updates, and refill notifications.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM: Required to trigger reminder alerts precisely at your scheduled dose times.
  • VIBRATE & WAKE_LOCK: Ensures notification alerts reliably sound and vibrate even when the device is locked or in sleep mode.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Re-schedules active medication reminders automatically whenever your device restarts.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required solely for Google Sign-In and cloud database synchronization.

8. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for unassisted use by children under the age of 13. While parents or legal guardians may create family sub-profiles to manage medications on behalf of their children, we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.
